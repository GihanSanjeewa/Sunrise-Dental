Staff Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Step-by-step user manual for clinic receptionists, dentists, and administrative personnel

1 Logging into the System

Open the login page and enter your assigned username and password. Staff roles determine your permissions:

  • ADMIN: Manage dentists, treatments pricing, user accounts, and system audits.
  • RECEPTIONIST: Register patients, book appointments, generate bills, and record payments.
  • DENTIST: Review assigned patients, complete visits, and enter clinical notes.
2 Registering a New Patient

Navigate to the Patients tab and click "Register New Patient". Fill in:

  • Full Legal Name, Residential Address, and Valid Mobile Phone Number.
  • Email Address, Date of Birth, and Gender.
  • The system automatically issues an immutable unique ID (e.g. P-000001).
3 Booking an Appointment

On the Appointments page, click "Book New Appointment":

  • Select the registered patient, desired dentist, and treatment procedure.
  • Select an appointment date and standard 30-minute time slot.
  • Double-Booking Prevention: If the dentist is already occupied at that slot, the system automatically blocks booking with: "Selected dentist is already booked for this time."
4 Searching Appointments

You can locate any patient visit quickly:

  • Direct Lookup: Enter the unique number (e.g. APT-2026-000001) in the top search bar to view comprehensive appointment and patient details.
  • Multi-Filter Search: Filter by date, attending dentist, or status (BOOKED, CONFIRMED, COMPLETED, CANCELLED).
5 Updating & Rescheduling

If a patient requests a schedule adjustment:

  • Click the Edit button next to the appointment.
  • Select the new date or time. The conflict validation engine verifies slot availability before saving.
6 Concluding a Visit & Clinical Notes

Once the dentist performs the oral treatment:

  • Update the appointment status to COMPLETED.
  • Enter clinical observations, procedure findings, or prescription notes.
  • This makes the appointment eligible for billing invoice creation.
7 Generating the Treatment Bill

Navigate to Billing & Payments and click "Generate Treatment Bill":

  • Select the completed appointment. Consultation fees and procedure costs are derived automatically from the database catalog.
  • Configure optional promotional discount % or statutory tax %.
  • The system generates a unique invoice: BILL-2026-000001.
8 & 9 Recording Payment & Printing Receipt

To settle the patient's invoice:

  • Click "Pay" on the pending bill row.
  • Choose payment method: CASH, CARD, or BANK_TRANSFER.
  • Click "Record & Generate Receipt". An official printable receipt appears immediately with a "Print Receipt" button that triggers browser printing.
10 & 11 Generating Reports & Secure Logout

Clinic administrators and receptionists can access the Reports & Analytics tab to view Daily Schedules, Monthly Trends, Dentist Utilization, and Financial Revenue. When your shift finishes, always click "Sign Out" at the bottom of the sidebar to invalidate your active session.
